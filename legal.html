1. Who we are

Season One LLC is a Washington limited liability company ("Season One," "we," "us," or "our"). Season One LLC owns and operates seasononehealthcare.com and publishes Season One — PA Exam Prep (the "App"), an offline study resource for physician assistant students. The App is a product of Season One LLC; it is not published by, licensed from, or operated on behalf of any other company, school, or certifying body.

This document is both our privacy policy and our terms of use, and it covers the website and the App together. By using either, you agree to it. If you do not agree, please do not use the site or the App.

2. Information we collect

Through this website. Only what you choose to submit: your email address if you request beta access or launch updates, and your name, email, and message if you write to us.

In the App. If you create an account, your email address, display name, and an encrypted representation of your password are stored on your device. You may optionally add your PA program, expected graduation date, current rotation, exam target date, and self-identified military or veteran status. Your practice sets, answers, scores, bookmarks, notes, and settings are recorded on your device as you study.

Submissions. If you send us a suggested correction to a question or a piece of study content, we receive the text of that submission and, only if you provide it, your email address.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health records, government identifiers, or payment information. The App contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking technologies, and it does not track you across other applications or websites. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not request or store employment or job-placement preferences.

4. How information is stored and used

Study data is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced through your own iCloud account so it is available on your other Apple devices. It is stored under your Apple ID, not on Season One's servers. We cannot see your study activity, your answers, or your performance. That information exists only to operate the App — to record your progress, build practice sets, and decide what to show you next.

Information you submit through this website is used to reply to you and, if you asked for it, to email you about the App's release and related updates. We do not use your information for advertising or for automated decision-making.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Study data is not shared with anyone: iCloud sync moves it between your own devices, with Apple acting as the infrastructure provider under your Apple ID, and Season One has no access to it at any point.

Website form submissions are processed by Web3Forms, and content submissions sent from the App are transmitted to our database provider, Supabase. Both act as service providers processing data on our instructions, not as independent recipients for their own purposes. We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of Season One or others.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stored in the App persists until you delete it. You may delete your account at any time from the App's Settings, which permanently removes your account, profile, and study data from that device and from your iCloud storage, so it is removed from your other devices as they sync. Deleting the App removes the copy on that device; if you also want the iCloud copy gone, delete your account first.

Every launch-update email includes an unsubscribe link. To request deletion of a content submission, or of information held from a website form, write to us at the address below. We will honor the request unless the submission has already been incorporated into published study content, which we cannot separate out after the fact.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because the App keeps your data on your own device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in Settings. For anything else, contact us and we will respond within the period applicable law requires. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

8. Children's privacy

The App and this site are intended for physician assistant students and other adults. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including on-device storage and encrypted transmission of anything you send us. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How this content is made

Season One LLC uses artificial intelligence as a drafting and research tool in the development of its study content and software. AI assists human work; it does not replace professional judgment.

Automated checks validate drug names against the National Library of Medicine's RxNorm database and confirm that every practice question is answerable from the study content in the App.

The App is a study aid, not a clinical reference, and its content may contain errors. Medicine also changes. Always verify against current clinical guidelines, your program's instruction, and primary sources before relying on anything you read here.
