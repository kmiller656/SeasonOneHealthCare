Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Season One LLC · Last updated August 2026

1. Introduction

Season One LLC, a Washington limited liability company ("Season One," "we," "us," or "our"), operates seasononehealthcare.com and provides Season One — PA Exam Prep (the "App"), a study resource for physician assistant students. This policy explains what information we collect through the website and the App, how it is used, and the choices available to you. By using this site or the App, you agree to this policy and to the Terms of Use set out below.

2. Information we collect

Through this website. Only what you choose to submit through its two forms: your email address if you sign up for launch updates, and your name, email, and message if you use the contact form.

In the App. If you create an account, your email address, display name, and an encrypted representation of your password are stored on your device. You may optionally provide your PA program, expected graduation date, current rotation, exam target date, and self-identified military or veteran status. Your practice sets, answers, scores, bookmarks, and settings are recorded as you study.

Submissions. If you choose to submit a suggested correction to a question, we receive the text of that submission and, only if you provide it, your email address.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your device location, contacts, photos, health records, government identifiers, or payment information. The App contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking technologies, and does not track you across other applications or websites. The App does not request or store employment or job-placement preferences.

4. How information is stored and used

App account, profile, and study data are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to Season One. We cannot see your study activity, your answers, or your performance. This information is used solely to operate the App — to record your progress, generate practice sets, and tailor which content is shown to you.

Information submitted through this website is used to respond to your message and, if you signed up for it, to email you about the App's launch and related updates. We do not use your information for advertising or automated decision-making.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Because App study data remains on your device, it is not shared with anyone.

Website form submissions are processed through Web3Forms, and question submissions are transmitted to our database provider, Supabase. Both act as service providers processing data on our behalf, not as independent recipients for their own purposes. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Season One or others.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored App data persists until you delete it. You may delete your account at any time from the App's Settings, which permanently removes your account, profile, and study data from your device. Uninstalling the App also removes this data.

Every launch-update email includes an unsubscribe link. To request deletion of a question submission or of information held from a website form, contact us at the address below. We will honor the request unless the submission has already been incorporated into published content.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because the App stores your data on your own device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in Settings. For anything else, contact us and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

The App and this site are intended for physician assistant students and other adults. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including on-device storage and encrypted transmission of submissions. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Content development and verification

Season One uses artificial intelligence as a drafting and research tool in the development of its study content and software. AI is used to assist human work, never to replace professional judgment.

Clinical content is verified by a licensed clinician before publication, and the software is reviewed by a qualified software engineer for correctness, security, and safe handling of user data. Automated checks additionally validate drug names against the National Library of Medicine's RxNorm database and confirm that every practice question is answerable from the study content in the App.

Medicine changes, and no review process is infallible. Always verify against current clinical guidelines and your program's instruction.
