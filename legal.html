Privacy & Terms

Draft — last updated August 2026

This is a draft, not a final policy

This page describes what the current version of this site actually does — a launch-notification signup and a general contact form. It has not yet been reviewed by an attorney and should not be relied on as a final, binding privacy policy or terms of use until it has.

Overview

Season One Healthcare ("Season One," "we," "us") operates seasononehealthcare.com to share information about Season One — PA Exam Prep, an iOS app for physician assistant students preparing for board exams, and to let visitors sign up for launch updates or get in touch with us. This page explains what information this website collects, why, and what choices you have. It covers the website; see "The app itself" below for how the app is different.

Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us through this site's two forms:

  • Get notified — your email address, so we can let you know when the app launches.
  • Contact form — your name, email, and your message, for whatever reason you're reaching out.

We do not ask for or knowingly collect health information, Social Security numbers, or payment details through this site.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to respond to your message and to email you about the app's launch and related updates if you signed up for that. We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Sharing & third parties

We don't sell your personal information. Form submissions are processed through Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service that routes your submission to our inbox — it's a data processor acting on our behalf, not an independent recipient of your data for its own purposes.

Your choices & unsubscribing

Every launch-update email will include an unsubscribe link. You can also email us at any time (see "Contact us" below) to be removed from any list or to ask what information we have on file about you and have it deleted, subject to any legitimate need to retain it.

The app itself

Season One — PA Exam Prep is built to work fully offline and does not require an account to use its core study features. It does not collect or transmit your study data to us. If that changes in a future version, the app's own in-app disclosures and its App Store privacy label will reflect it — this website's policy covers the website, not the app's on-device behavior.

Independent study resource, not medical advice

Season One — PA Exam Prep is an independent, additional study resource for physician assistant students. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NCCPA; PANCE® and EOR™ are trademarks of their respective owners. Nothing in the app or on this site is medical advice, and the app makes no claims or promises about exam outcomes, pass rates, or scores. Content is written to publicly available exam blueprints and is not yet reviewed by a licensed clinician — always verify against current clinical guidelines.
