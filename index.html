Season One — PA Exam Prep is an independent iPhone study app for physician assistant students: board-style practice questions with a rationale for every answer, a clinical reference library of 972 conditions, and a medication reference — all mapped to the official PANCE and PAEA end-of-rotation blueprints, and all of it working offline.

A look inside the PANCE prep app.

Real screens from the current beta build — no mockups.

Everything you need for the PANCE and your EOR exams.

An independent study companion — not a replacement for your PA program, but a focused tool for board review.

Board-style question bank

Over 2,100 practice questions across all six EOR rotations, each with a written rationale explaining the correct answer and why the tempting distractors are wrong.

Practice by rotation and blueprint section

Filter practice sets by Family Medicine, Emergency Medicine, Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, Psychiatry or Women's Health — then drill into a specific PAEA blueprint section like Cardiovascular or Dermatologic.

Clinical reference library

972 conditions covering pathophysiology, clinical presentation, diagnosis and treatment, with verified clinical images for dermatology, ECG rhythms and musculoskeletal findings.

Medication reference

139 drug classes organized by body system, so you can review pharmacology alongside the condition you are studying.

Tutor & Exam modes

Tutor mode explains after every question. Exam mode holds feedback until the end and scores you, so you can simulate real testing conditions.

Spaced repetition & progress

Missed questions resurface sooner and mastered ones stretch out. A mastery dashboard shows where you stand by rotation and organ system.

Works completely offline

Every question, condition and image is bundled with the app. Study on rotation, in a call room, or on a plane — no connection needed.

No account required

The app opens straight to studying. No email, no password, no profile. Your progress stays on your device and is never uploaded.

Free during the beta

Everything above is free for beta testers. In exchange we ask for honest feedback on question quality — including where we fall short.

Common questions

Is the PANCE prep app really free?

Yes. It is completely free during the beta, with no trial limit, no paywalled sections and no ads.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. The app opens directly to the home screen. There is no sign-up, no email required, and no data leaves your device.

Does it cover end-of-rotation exams as well as the PANCE?

Yes. Questions and conditions are mapped to the PAEA end-of-rotation topic lists for all six core rotations, so you can study for an upcoming EOR exam specifically.

Does the app work without internet?

Every question, condition, medication and image ships inside the app, so it works fully offline.

Is this affiliated with the NCCPA?

No. Season One — PA Exam Prep is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NCCPA or PAEA.
