Everything you need in one place.

An independent, additional study resource — not a replacement for your program, but a focused companion for the boards.

Board-style question bank

Thousands of clinical-vignette practice questions across every EOR rotation, with a rationale for every answer.

Full condition library

Over 1,000 conditions covering pathophysiology, symptoms, diagnosis, and treatment, written to the official PANCE and EOR blueprints.

Practice by rotation

Filter practice sets by Internal Medicine, Family Medicine, Emergency Medicine, Pediatrics, Psychiatry, or Women's Health.

Tutor & Exam modes

Tutor mode explains as you go; Exam mode holds feedback until the end, so you can simulate the real thing.

Spaced repetition

Missed questions resurface sooner, mastered ones stretch out — so your review time goes where it's needed.

Studies fully offline

No connection required. Study on rotations, on a plane, wherever you are.
