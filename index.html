FAQ

Common questions

Is the PANCE prep app really free?

Yes — completely free during the beta. No trial limit, no paywalled sections, no ads.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. The app opens directly to the home screen. No sign-up, no email, and no data leaves your device.

Does it cover end-of-rotation exams as well as the PANCE?

Yes. Questions and conditions map to the PAEA end-of-rotation topic lists for all six core rotations.

Does the app work without internet?

Every question, condition, medication and image ships inside the app, so it works fully offline.

Is this affiliated with the NCCPA?

No. It is an independent study aid, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NCCPA or PAEA.
