Before you upload anything

Uploading lecture slides, handouts or a syllabus means you are asserting that your program holds the right to do so, and that you remain responsible for that material and for anything in it that identifies a patient or a person.

What we do with it: files go to a private bucket, are served only through short-lived signed links, and are readable by your program's staff and nobody else's. They are used to work out which lessons a plan should cover. They are not used to train anything, and they are not shared.

Upload and the AI reading step are being built. This page will not accept files until the agreement above is presented as a step you actively accept, rather than as text you scrolled past.
