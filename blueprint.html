Blueprint coverage

The end-of-rotation exams are built from a published blueprint, and every task area in it carries a weight. This is that blueprint, rotation by rotation, with the exact number of Season One conditions, questions and full-depth lessons sitting behind each line. Nothing here is rounded up.

950 conditions 1900 questions 600 at full depth 6 rotations

How to read this

Weight is the share of that rotation's exam the task area accounts for. Conditions and Questions are what ships in the app today. Full depth counts the lessons rewritten to the deeper standard: mechanism, risk factors, laboratory findings, published criteria where they exist, a lined treatment plan, and a closing High Yield PANCE page.

A condition can be placed in more than one rotation, because the blueprints overlap. Cardiovascular disease is tested in Family Medicine, Internal Medicine and Emergency Medicine alike, so the same condition is counted once under each. The rotation totals therefore add to more than 950, which is the number of distinct conditions in the app.

Depth is uneven and the numbers say so. Dermatology, hematology and endocrine are all but finished, and infectious disease and neurology are well along. Growth and development is the first task area written in full. Musculoskeletal, EENOT and gastrointestinal are now the thin ones among the large areas, and are what the current pass is working through. No task area is left at zero, though human sexuality is barely started.
