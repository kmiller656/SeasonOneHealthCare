Season One for PA programs.

The same library your students would buy for themselves, licensed for the whole cohort — with a place for you to put the material you already hand out, and a straight answer to whether they have done it.

What your program gets

Seats for the cohort

Every student gets the full app: 950 conditions, 950 lessons, and 1,900 board-style questions with a written rationale on every answer. It works entirely offline, which matters on a rotation with no signal.

Assign your own material

Upload the lecture PDFs and handouts you already use, and assign them — to one student, to a cohort, or to the whole roster. It lands in the app they are already studying in rather than a folder they forget.

See who has done the work

Assignment status, lessons completed, and question accuracy by rotation and blueprint section. Enough to find the student who has gone quiet three weeks before the exam does.

Your program's colors

Your name, your logo and your color inside the app, so it reads as something the program provides rather than one more subscription a student found.

Built against the blueprint, not around it

Every condition is placed against the PAEA® end-of-rotation topic lists and the NCCPA® PANCE® blueprint, so a set can be filtered to the rotation your students are actually on. Six end-of-rotation exams are covered plus the PANCE itself, and the same library carries through all three years — nothing has to be re-bought for boards.

You can see exactly what is covered, task area by task area, on the blueprint page.

Where this stands, honestly

The student app is real and in beta today. The library, the lessons, the question bank and the offline behavior are all shipping and can be demonstrated on request.

The program side — rosters, assignments and faculty visibility — is in active development. We are building it with a small number of founding programs so that it matches how a PA program actually runs rather than how we imagine it does. If that is interesting rather than disqualifying, this is the right moment to talk: founding partners set the requirements, get preferential pricing, and are not charged for what has not shipped.

On privacy, the line we hold: faculty see progress, never a student's private work. Notes, highlights and tutor conversations stay the student's own, and what a program can see is disclosed to the student before they join it.
