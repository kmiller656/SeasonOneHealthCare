For students

Bring Season One to your class

Season One is built by one PA student. The fastest way it gets better is a cohort using it at once and telling us what broke. If that could be your class, this is the ask.

What you do

Run one session for your cohort — a lunch hour, a study-group slot, twenty minutes at the end of a review session. Walk them through the app, get them signed up, and collect what they think. That is it. We will send you whatever you need to run it, and you never have to sell anything.

What your class gets

Extended free access for everyone who attends, past the end of the open beta, so your cohort keeps it through boards whatever pricing ends up being.

A group rate for your cohort when pricing arrives, scaled to how many of you there are. Bigger class, better rate.

Their rotation built next. If your program hits Surgery before we have finished it, an organising class moves up the queue.

What you get

A direct line to the person writing it. Not a support queue — the developer. If your cohort says a rotation is thin, that is what gets worked on next.

Early access to features before they ship, and a say in what they look like.

Something real to put on a CV. We will write you a reference describing exactly what you did, and you are credited on the site if you want to be.
